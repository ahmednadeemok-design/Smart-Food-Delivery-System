# Smart Food Delivery Platform for Narowal

This project is scoped as a Narowal city smart food delivery platform rather than a Pakistan-wide generic app.

The demo dataset focuses on real Narowal restaurant names and local zones such as Main Bazaar, Circular Road,
College Road, Railway Road, Zafarwal Road, Shakargarh Road, Pasrur Road, and UET Narowal. Restaurant locations
use approximate coordinates around Narowal city center for map, rider route, heatmap, and delivery simulation.

Seed restaurants include Palmer Restaurant, Buddy's Narowal, The Dining Family Restaurant, City Restaurant Narowal,
Moon Grill Restaurant, Lasani Al Khan Restaurant, Crunch Bite Family Restaurant, Anbala Sweets, Bakers and Cash &
Carry, ZFC Narowal, and Virsa Restaurant Narowal.
